# Brand marks

`Bare-marks.vectorcraft` has four artboards: debossed for light and for dark
surfaces, embossed emerald and embossed white. The mark fills about 75% of a
transparent square, leaving room for edge shading. There is no tile or circle
behind it.

The app loads them through `RCTheme brandMarkImage:size:grey:` and
`drawBrandMark:inRect:grey:`:

- Empty chat: `rc-mark-debossed-light/dark@2x.png`, picked by appearance. A
  temporary chat uses the grey variant.
- Launch and welcome screens on dark linen: `rc-mark-embossed-white@2x.png`.
- Raised green variant: `rc-mark-embossed-emerald@2x.png` (available in the kit
  gallery).

`rc-app-icon-*` are the small rounded-tile icons used in app headers.
`Tumblr-strong-shadow.vectorcraft` is the master of the selected Home Screen icon.
